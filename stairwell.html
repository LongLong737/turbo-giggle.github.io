SURVIVAL PROTOCOL

Watch your step.

01 / Creak

Creak quietly stalks when unwatched and backs away when spotted. Brief peeks scare it off. Its hands lower as staring or crowding raises aggression. If its left hand reaches into view, turn around before it strikes. Never walk back through it.

02 / Noise

Noise retraces your footsteps and pauses when you stop. If you get far ahead, it fast-forwards. Avoid crossing your earlier path. Circle safely to its own TV and press E to push it down the stairs; contact is fatal.

03 / Meld

The growth blocks the stairs. Find the alarm in the side room, pull it, and reach the marked fire shelter within 15 seconds. Wait for the sprinklers to stop.

04 / The Depot

Collect items and grind them into scrap. Cobbler exchanges scrap for gold at Landing 199. Gold buys batteries and bandages.

05 / Stems & garages

Carry a Stem with E and throw with Q. Sprinklers fire it into ceramic you can grind. A garage key opens one cache.

06 / Equipment

F toggles your light; R recharges a Shakelight. Shift sprints, B looks behind, C crouches, and E interacts. On touch, use the left stick and drag the right side to look. Gamepad: sticks move/look, A interacts, B toggles light, RT sprints, X recharges, Y throws, and LB looks behind.
